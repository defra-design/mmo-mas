Application details

Application name
Installation of safety ladders at Brixham Fish Quay
Proposed works summary

We are applying for a marine licence to replace the safety ladders on the north and east walls of Brixham Fish Quay. We will remove 4 corroded galvanised ladders and fix 6 new stainless steel ladders to the quay wall. Two of the old ladders have already been taken out of use, which leaves long stretches of the quay with no safe way for crews to get on and off their boats, or for anyone who falls in to climb out.

The new ladders will be bolted to the face of the existing wall with resin anchors, working from the quay and a small harbour workboat at low tide. No piling, dredging or work on the seabed is needed.

Preferred start and end dates for the licence
February 2027 to April 2027
