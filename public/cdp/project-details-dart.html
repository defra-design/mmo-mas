Application details

Application name
Dart estuary navigation buoy replacement
Proposed works summary
We are applying for a marine licence to replace 6 navigation buoys that mark the edges of the main channel past our mooring trots at the mouth of the River Dart. Three are on the Kingswear side and three on the Dartmouth side. The existing steel buoys are corroded and their lights are unreliable. We will replace each one in the same position with a new plastic buoy with a solar-powered light, on a new concrete sinker and chain. The work will be done from a workboat with a lifting crane and will take about 2 weeks.
Preferred start and end dates for the licence
January 2027 to March 2027
