Yes

Details of the special legal powers.

Torbay Council is the statutory harbour authority for Tor Bay, which includes Brixham Harbour, and manages it as Tor Bay Harbour Authority. The ladders are being installed by the harbour authority on its own quay, so no other harbour consent is needed.

Are your proposed works located in a harbour authority area?

Application guidance: A harbour authority is an organisation with legal powers to manage a harbour, and includes municipal, private and trust ports.

Yes

Details of the harbour authority.

Tor Bay Harbour Authority (Torbay Council). We are the harbour authority and the applicant. The works have been agreed by the Harbour Master and will be published to harbour users as a notice to mariners before work starts. Internal ref: TBHA/ENG/2026/118.

Yes

Torbay Council Planning – the planning team confirmed by email on 22 September 2026 that the ladders are permitted development for a statutory undertaker and do not need planning permission. Because the quay is in the Brixham Conservation Area we also checked with the council’s conservation officer, who had no concerns about the design.

Have you consulted with any public groups or organisations before making this application?

Application guidance: This could include relevant local groups in the community or organisations, for example, resident committees, local environmental and conservation groups, Companies House or Natural England.

Yes

Details of who you consulted and what advice they gave.

We have spoken to the fish quay users at the quarterly harbour liaison meeting in September 2026. They agreed the 6 ladder positions and asked us to avoid working on the quay edge during early morning landings, which we have built into our working hours. We also asked the local RNLI station for their views, and they supported the ladders being placed next to the busiest berths.
