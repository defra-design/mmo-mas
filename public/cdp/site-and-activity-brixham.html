Site 1 – Activity 1

Type of activity
Construction of new works
Sub-activities
  • Other: safety ladders fixed to an existing quay wall
Activity description
Removal of 4 corroded galvanised steel ladders and installation of 6 new marine grade 316 stainless steel safety ladders on the north and east walls of the fish quay. Each ladder is about 6 metres long, 450 millimetres wide, and reaches from the top of the quay to below the lowest astronomical tide. The ladders will be fixed to the wall with stainless steel brackets held by resin anchors drilled into the stonework, with grab handles on the quay top. Drilling will be done at low tide, above the water line, using hand held electric drills with dust collection. Work will be done from the quay edge and from the harbour workboat. Old ladders will be cut off and taken away for recycling. No piling, dredging or work on the seabed is needed.
