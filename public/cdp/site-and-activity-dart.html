Site 1 – Activity 1

Type of activity
Maintenance of existing works
What is being maintained
Navigation buoys
Activity description
Like for like replacement of 3 green starboard-hand navigation buoys on the east side of the main channel, in the South marine plan area. Each corroded steel buoy, its concrete sinker and its chain will be lifted out and replaced in the same position with a polyethylene buoy fitted with a solar-powered green light, a new 2 tonne concrete sinker and a new ground chain and riser. The sinkers are lowered straight onto the seabed by the workboat crane. No dredging or levelling of the seabed is needed. The old buoys, sinkers and chains will be taken ashore for recycling.
Maximum duration of activity
1 week
Completion date
Yes – before the start of the sailing season on 1 April 2027
Activity limited to specific months
Yes – January to March only, before salmon and sea trout smolts migrate down the river in April and May.
Proposed working hours
Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 16:00, around slack water.
