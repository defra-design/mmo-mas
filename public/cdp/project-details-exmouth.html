Application details

Application name
Exmouth Marina pontoon replacement
Proposed works summary

Exe Estuary Marinas Ltd owns and runs Exmouth Marina, which has 172 berths for leisure boats, the Exmouth water taxi and visiting yachts. We are applying for a marine licence to replace the marina's floating pontoons and guide piles, which were installed in the 1990s and are now near the end of their life.

The old timber pontoons will be taken out and replaced with new aluminium framed pontoons on concrete floats, in the same layout. The 22 steel guide piles that hold the pontoons in place will be pulled out and replaced with 22 new piles in the same positions. The work will be done from the quayside and from a piling barge inside the marina basin.

The works will be paid for by a loan of £2.15 million, which we will repay through a new berth tariff of £585 per metre per year from April 2028, up from £472 today. The contract price agreed with our pontoon supplier is £1.68 million. These figures were shared with berth holders in confidence and have not yet been made public.

Preferred start and end dates for the licence
October 2027 to March 2028
