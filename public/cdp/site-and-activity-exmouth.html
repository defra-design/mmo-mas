Site 1 – Activity 1

Type of activity
Maintenance of existing works
What is being maintained
Pontoons
Activity description
Removal of about 410 metres of timber floating pontoons, including the main walkway and 9 finger arms, and replacement with new aluminium framed pontoons on concrete floats in the same layout. The new pontoon units will be built off site, brought in by road and lifted into the water from the quayside by a mobile crane. Old pontoons will be lifted out the same way and taken away by road for recycling. The access bridge from the quay will be reused.
Maximum duration of activity
5 months
Completion date
Not needed to be completed by a certain date
Activity limited to specific months
Yes – October to March only, when most berth holders have lifted their boats out for the winter. Piling only on the rising tide, when the mudflats next to the marina are covered.
Proposed working hours
Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00. No piling before 09:00 or after 16:30.

Site 1 – Activity 2

Type of activity
Construction of new works
Sub-activities
  • Piling
Activity description
Removal of 22 corroded steel guide piles and installation of 22 new 457 millimetre diameter tubular steel guide piles in the same positions. The old piles will be pulled out with a vibrating hammer from a piling barge. The new piles will be driven with a vibrating hammer, with an impact hammer used only for the last metre of each pile if needed, using a soft start. No more than 2 piles will be driven in a day.
Maximum duration of activity
6 weeks
Completion date
Not needed to be completed by a certain date
Activity limited to specific months
Yes – October to March only, when most berth holders have lifted their boats out for the winter. Piling only on the rising tide, when the mudflats next to the marina are covered.
Proposed working hours
Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00. No piling before 09:00 or after 16:30.
