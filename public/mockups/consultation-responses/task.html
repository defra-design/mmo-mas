Help with extending the deadline

Only extend the deadline if a consultee has asked for more time. You can extend it more than once, and after it has passed.

  • The new deadline applies to every consultee on this case, not just the one who asked.
  • Consultees are not sent a notification. Let the consultee who asked know the new deadline yourself.
  • You can see who has responded on the Consultation responses tab.
