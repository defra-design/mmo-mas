Help with extending the deadline

Extend the deadline if a consultee asks for more time, or if you need longer. You can extend it more than once, and after it has passed.

  • The new deadline applies to every consultee on this case, not just the one who asked.
  • Consultees are not sent a notification. If a consultee asked for more time, let them know the new deadline yourself.
  • You can see who has responded on the Consultation responses tab.
