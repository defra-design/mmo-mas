Marine plan policies supporting documents

Applicants can upload supporting documents and link each one to the marine plan policies it supports, saying where in the document the relevant information is.

For MVP, caseworkers see every document on a single Supporting documents page in the Marine plan policies tab. These are the other ways we explored of showing the documents against individual policies, which we decided were not MVP.

Marine plan policies tab – MLA/2026/10015

  • Documents within each policy

    Each policy lists its documents under the applicant's consideration. Opens on Biodiversity, where South West Biodiversity 1 has two documents.

Policy assessment – South West Biodiversity 1

  • Documents as D365 fields

    Each document gets its own read-only fields: the document, its type and where to find the evidence.

  • Documents as injected HTML

    One Supporting documents row with no padlock, like WFD's Assessment provided, laid out as on the Marine plan policies tab.
