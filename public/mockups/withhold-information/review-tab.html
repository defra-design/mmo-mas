Withholding information from the public register

Do you want to ask us to withhold any information from the public register?
Reason
Tell us what you want withheld and why
We would like the fees and financial figures in our activity description withheld. A second operator is expected to bid for a concession in the same harbour next year, and if they can see what we are paying and what we expect to take they can pitch against us. We would also like the pontoon dimensions and fixing method shown on our construction drawing withheld. We spent two years getting this set-up right and we do not want another hire business copying it.
Do you have approval from the Secretary of State for Defence to withhold information for national security reasons?
Tell us what you want withheld
Royal Navy patrol vessels will berth at the outer end of the pontoon overnight. We would like the out-of-hours mooring times in our activity description withheld, along with the positions of the CCTV and access control equipment shown on our construction drawing. We have approval from the Secretary of State for Defence, reference MOD-SOS-2026-0412, dated 3 August 2026.
