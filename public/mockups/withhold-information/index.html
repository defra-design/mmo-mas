Withhold information variations

The application form may ask two separate questions: whether the applicant wants information withheld for commercial or industrial confidentiality reasons, and whether they want it withheld for national security reasons. Each Yes comes with the applicant's explanation of what they want withheld and why.

Because MAS then already knows which grounds were raised, the Public register task no longer needs the caseworker to say what the request relates to. Each ground has its own section, commercial first, and the caseworker only makes a decision where the applicant answered Yes.

Public register task – MLA/2026/10015

  • Yes to commercial confidentiality, No to national security

    A decision to make on commercial or industrial confidentiality only.

  • No to commercial confidentiality, Yes to national security

    A decision to make on national security only. The applicant gives their Secretary of State approval in their explanation.

  • Yes to both

    A decision to make on each ground, each with its own rationale and message to the applicant.

  • No to both

    Nothing to assess. Only the personal information check is left to do.
