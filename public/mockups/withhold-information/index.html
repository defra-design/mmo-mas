Withhold information variations

The applicant now answers one Yes or No question up front, then selects one reason. For commercial or industrial confidentiality they explain what they want withheld and why. For national security they must already have approval from the Secretary of State for Defence. Without it, they cannot enter anything and the information is published.

The mockup plays this back on the task, which is renamed from Public register to Withholding information. Use the bar at the top of the page to switch between the applicant's possible answers. The form underneath can be clicked and filled in.
