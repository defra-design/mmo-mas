Iteration 2 – design overview

Iteration 2 develops the receive and assess journey from reviewing an application into recording assessment decisions and preparing the case for consultation. It adds individual marine plan policy assessments, consultation preparation, public register decisions, public notice and evidence review, and a task to start consultation. It also explores how caseworkers reject an application or arrange a transfer to MCMS.

This overview describes the current prototype, including the latest additions to Iteration 2. It builds on the Iteration 1 design overview.

Different ways of displaying tasks and marine plan policy assessments

Iteration 2 explores four ways of organising the case officer’s work. Each uses the same individual policy assessment form; the difference is where the tasks and policy assessments sit, and how the officer moves between reviewing the application and assessing it.

  • MLA/2026/10015 – dedicated Tasks tab

    The Tasks tab is the first thing the case officer sees when entering the case. It shows the full-width Tasks grid, with the marine plan policy assessment grid underneath. All assessment work is brought together in one place, with no Tasks side panel.

    The separate Marine plan policies tab remains available for reviewing the applicant’s policy answers, grouped by sector. The officer can review related answers there and open individual assessment records from the Tasks tab.

  • MLA/2026/10014 – persistent Tasks panel and assessments on the MPP tab

    The Tasks panel stays visible on the right while the officer moves between tabs. The Marine plan policies tab contains the full-width assessment grid, replacing the separate applicant-answer view. Opening a policy brings its applicant information and the officer’s assessment together on one form. Save and close returns to the MPP tab to choose the next policy.

  • MLA/2026/10013 – assessments below Case summary and a persistent Tasks panel

    The Tasks panel stays visible on the right. The full-width marine plan policy assessment grid sits below the Case summary, allowing the officer to open each policy to review the applicant’s answer and record a decision. The separate Marine plan policies tab retains the read-only applicant answers.

  • MLA/2026/10012 – tasks and policy assessments in the side panel

    The persistent right-hand panel contains the Tasks list followed by a second list of individual marine plan policy assessments. Policies appear in a compact, paginated list rather than a full-width grid. The Marine plan policies tab retains the read-only applicant answers.

Detailed exploration: ML-1329 – marine plan policy assessments and layout variants.

Changes to applicant information

  • Application details

    Uses “Application details” and “Application name” in place of the earlier project labels. It includes the proposed works summary and displays preferred licence dates at month and year level.

  • Sites and activities

    MLA/2026/10002 adds an example with multiple sites and activities, keeping each activity associated with its site. Sub-activities are listed explicitly, and construction drawing links sit alongside the relevant site information.

  • WFD, Other permissions and Public register

    Refines the applicant question wording, Yes/No answers and document links. Public register examples show both an applicant request to withhold information and a case where nothing has been requested for withholding.

Assessment tasks

  • Site check

    Refines the existing task with separate sections for coordinates and shape, the WFD assessment area, and optional notes. Required answers are checked on Save and close, which now unlocks the additional assessment tasks introduced in this iteration.

  • Water Framework Directive

    Adds a required “Record your considerations” field when the officer answers Yes to “Is the WFD section complete and acceptable?”. The field is hidden and not required for a No answer. The applicant question wording and assessment-document link have also been refined.

  • Marine plan policy assessments

    There are 41 policy assessments across four groups: Cross-cutting, Economic, Environmental and Social. Each policy has its own record, showing the policy group, policy information and applicant’s consideration. The caseworker records an outcome of Compliant, Non-compliant or Consultation required, with a required reason.

    The policy grid shows Policy, Group and Status, with sorting and filtering. Task status is separate from the assessment outcome: a completed assessment is Done, whichever outcome was recorded. Assessments open read-only until Site check is complete, then become To do.

  • Prepare for consultation

    The caseworker selects organisations from a searchable lookup and chooses what each should receive. Application notification tells an organisation about the application without asking for a response. Request for advice reveals notes for explaining what advice is needed.

    Selecting an organisation reveals the next blank row, so the list grows as needed. Each selected organisation needs a consultation type. The caseworker can save valid work as In progress, or mark it complete and save it as Done.

  • Public register

    Sits directly below Site check, bringing withholding decisions early enough for the applicant to receive a response and decide whether to continue. The officer reviews the applicant’s request and assesses commercial or industrial confidentiality, national security, or both. They can agree in full, agree in part or disagree, recording a rationale and, for partial agreement or refusal, a message for the applicant.

    A personal-information check always appears, including when no withholding request was made. The officer then follows a link to the separate redaction journey in a new tab. Required fields must be completed before saving: ticking the completion checkbox makes the task Done; leaving it unticked saves it as In progress.

    The examples also compare guidance: MLA/2026/10014 displays it openly with information icons, while MLA/2026/10015 uses collapsed “Help with…” accordions. MLA/2026/10013 shows no withholding request, so the assessment section is omitted and personal-information guidance is in an accordion.

    More detail: ML-1381 – Public register task and ML-1520 – task structure and guidance variants.

  • Redact the application

    The separate redaction journey shows the submitted application and the applicant’s withholding request. The officer edits a copy of an answer for publication while preserving the original, using a consistent redaction marker. They can withhold a site’s coordinates and map together, withhold documents, or upload redacted replacement documents. Redactions can also be changed or removed.

    A preview opens in a new tab to show the public version, with withheld information omitted. Save redactions records the work and checks that no answers remain open for editing. This prepares the application for publication; publishing happens back in MAS.

    More detail: ML-1383 – redacting answers, locations and documents.

  • Public notice

    The caseworker decides whether a site notice is required. If none is needed, they record why and the task becomes Done. If a site notice is needed, they write its summary using the applicant’s proposed works as a reference, and identify whether marine users, community users or both need to be told.

    Saving the site-notice requirement changes the task to Awaiting applicant, representing the handover for the applicant to display the notice and provide evidence.

  • Review public notice evidence and resubmissions

    When the applicant submits evidence, the Public notice task becomes Done and a separate Review public notice evidence task appears as To do. For each location, the officer sees the display date and links to a close-up photograph and a photograph of the surroundings. They accept or reject each location separately, with required comments explaining any rejection.

    Save and close checks the decisions and comments. All accepted means Done; any rejection means Awaiting applicant. The intended applicant journey asks for replacement photographs only for rejected locations. Resubmission reuses the existing review task, showing Resubmitted - to review, with earlier evidence and decisions retained as read-only history. The accept/reject cycle repeats until the evidence is satisfactory.

    MLA/2026/10015 shows the stage before evidence arrives; MLA/2026/10014 shows the initial review; MLA/2026/10013 shows replacement photographs for Location 1, with Locations 2 and 3 already accepted.

    More detail: ML-1544 – receiving and reviewing public notice evidence.

  • Start consultation

    Appears in the task list from the outset as Cannot start yet, opening read-only until the required assessment work is complete. Once ready, the caseworker confirms that consultation can start and selects Save and close. The case status changes to Consultation and the task becomes Done and read-only.

    The task explains the intended publication of the application and sending of the notifications and advice requests prepared earlier. Outstanding site-notice evidence does not prevent consultation starting once the notice has been prepared. The index includes a shortcut that loads a ready-to-start example.

Case actions

  • Reject application

    A command-bar action opens a dialog for selecting the application sections with issues and writing the details the applicant needs. Both are required. Confirming changes the case status to Rejected and records the reasons, explanation, person and date in a read-only section on the Case summary.

  • Transfer to MCMS

    A two-step command-bar flow separates requesting a transfer from completing it. The caseworker first records the reasons, changing the status to Transfer pending. The second step represents the Business Support Team recording the MCMS reference after the manual transfer, changing the status to Transferred. The Case summary retains the request and completion details.

Task form and status changes

Task forms use clearer sections, consistent required markers and padlocks, and Save and close commands. Required-field checks show a form-level message and feedback beside the affected fields. Tasks that cannot start yet can now be inspected read-only, with a notification explaining the prerequisite.

Rejection, transfer and consultation actions update the status in both the Case list and case header.

Prototype scope

The examples illustrate different stages of the journey using prepared application and evidence data. The redaction summary describes the separate journey documented in ML-1383. External notifications, public-register publication and transfer into MCMS are represented rather than carried out by this prototype.

The current Start consultation gate checks the required tasks and two policy assessments (SW-CC-1 and SW-CC-3) as a prototype shortcut. The ready-to-start example fills all policy assessments. This shortcut does not define the final service rule.
